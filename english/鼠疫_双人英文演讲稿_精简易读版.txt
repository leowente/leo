鼠疫双人英文演讲稿（精简易读版）

使用说明：选用与你的 PPT 对应的一版，不要连续朗读两版。A、B 换成自己的名字。方括号和中文说明不朗读。

本稿比原稿更短。按中等语速、加换页停顿，每人约 2 分钟，全程约 4 分钟；不再按每人 3 分钟设计。

========================================

版本一：对应《鼠疫_蓝白模板版.pptx》（7 页）

A：213 词；B：199 词。

[Speaker A · 第 1 页]

Good morning, teachers and classmates. I'm A.
Today, my partner and I will talk about plague.
Look at this mouse. Isn't it cute?
But some wild mice and rats, and the fleas on them, can carry plague bacteria.
Of course, not every mouse has the disease.

[Speaker A · 第 2 页]

Plague is a serious disease caused by bacteria called Yersinia pestis.
It caused the Black Death hundreds of years ago.
Many people died because they had no effective medicine.
Today, plague still exists, but we can treat it.

[Speaker A · 第 3 页]

How does plague spread?
A flea bites an infected animal and picks up the bacteria.
Then it bites a person and passes on the bacteria.
People can also get sick through contact with an infected animal's blood or body tissues.
Pneumonic plague can spread between people through cough droplets during close contact.

[Speaker A · 第 4 页]

There are three main types of plague.
The first is bubonic plague. It causes swollen and painful lymph nodes.
The second is septicemic plague. It affects the blood and can cause serious bleeding.
The third is pneumonic plague. It affects the lungs and can make breathing difficult.
This type can spread between people through cough droplets during close contact.
All three types need quick treatment.

[交接给 B]

Now, my partner will talk about treatment and how to stay safe.

[Speaker B · 第 5 页]

Thank you, A. Hello everyone, I'm B.

People with plague may suddenly have a fever, headache, and feel very weak.
Other illnesses can cause these signs too.
If they appear after a flea bite or contact with a sick animal, see a doctor at once.
Tell the doctor what happened.
Antibiotics can treat plague.
Early treatment can save lives, but waiting too long can be deadly.
Do not try to treat it yourself.

[Speaker B · 第 6 页]

How can we stay safe?
First, do not touch sick or dead wild animals.
Second, avoid flea bites. Wear long sleeves and use insect repellent as directed.
Third, ask a vet how to keep fleas off your pets.
Keep food covered and remove rubbish around your home to avoid attracting rats.
If you have close contact with someone who has pneumonic plague, ask a doctor for advice.

[Speaker B · 第 7 页]

Plague is dangerous, but we know much more about it today.
Early treatment and good habits can help protect us.
If we find a dead wild animal, we should leave it alone and report it.
If we feel sick after possible contact, we should get medical help quickly.
Let's respect wildlife and follow medical advice.
Thank you for listening!
