使用说明
两版任选一版，不要连续朗读。A负责第1—3页；B负责第4—5页。
A约214词；B约210词。按每分钟115—125词，加换页、指图及交接停顿，每人约2分钟，全程约4分钟。实际时间请以排练为准。
所有中文、方括号和页码均不朗读。将A和B换成自己的名字。
本版沿用原txt的开场、传播、三种类型及预防表达，按新PPT重新组织，并补充疫情案例。

[Speaker A · 第1页 · The plague · 约30秒]

Good morning, teachers and classmates. I'm A. Today, my partner and I will talk about plague. Look at this mouse. Isn't it cute? But some wild mice and rats, and the fleas on them, can carry plague bacteria. Of course, not every mouse has the disease. How can a small flea cause such a serious problem?

[Speaker A · 第2页 · What is plague? How does it spread? · 约35秒]

Plague is a serious disease caused by bacteria called Yersinia pestis. It is not caused by a virus. Look at the three boxes. A flea bites an infected animal and picks up the bacteria. Then it bites a person and passes them on. People can also get sick through contact with an infected animal's blood or body tissues. Pneumonic plague can spread through cough droplets during close contact.

[Speaker A · 第3页 · Three main forms of plague · 约35秒，结束后交接]

There are three main types of plague. Bubonic plague causes swollen and painful lymph nodes. It is the most common type. Septicemic plague affects the blood and can cause shock and serious bleeding. Pneumonic plague affects the lungs and can make breathing difficult. These are different forms of the same disease. All three types need quick treatment. People may also have a sudden fever, headache, and feel very weak. A doctor must check these signs carefully. Now, my partner will show us where plague has happened and why it matters.

[Speaker B · 第4页 · Plague: then and now · 约50秒]

Thank you, A. Hello everyone, I'm B. Plague caused the Black Death hundreds of years ago. Many people died because they had no effective medicine. It also affected China. During the outbreak in northeast China in nineteen ten and nineteen eleven, about sixty thousand people died. Doctor Wu Lien-teh helped lead the fight against it. Plague still exists today. In Madagascar in twenty seventeen, an outbreak affected many people. By November twenty-second, over two thousand cases had been reported, including suspected cases. These examples show that plague is part of both history and modern life.

[Speaker B · 第5页 · Real outbreaks. Real harm. · 约55秒]

Look at this picture from our original presentation. It is an illustration of an old town, not a real outbreak photo. It helps us think about the fear that disease can bring. Plague can damage the lungs, cause serious bleeding, and even lead to death. Outbreaks also put pressure on hospitals and families. But there is hope. Antibiotics can treat plague, and early treatment can save lives. Do not touch sick or dead wild animals. Protect yourself from flea bites. If you feel sick after possible contact, get medical help quickly and tell the doctor what happened. Do not try to treat it yourself. Let's respect wildlife and follow medical advice. Thank you for listening!

排练提示
第1页：指向老鼠照片，不要把它说成感染鼠疫的老鼠。
第2页：按箭头指向动物、跳蚤和人。
第3页：疾病名称放慢读；结束后交给B。
第4页：数字用英文单词读法；“reported cases”包含疑似、可能和确诊病例。
第5页：指向历史场景插图，说明是示意图，然后讲危害和防治。
